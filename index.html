Search accepts field filters: game:fantasy stage:primed tag:hero painter:me, plus any free text. Keys: name, game, faction, unit, tag, stage, status, painter, owner, scale, material, paint, kit, note.

Photos: drag files onto the photo box. Order them around the mini to get the 360° spin, GIF and photo scan. Drag cards between columns in Paint Queue to change stage. Battle Log tracks games per army; Gallery is the photo wall.
